<!-- ===================== RESEARCH ===================== -->

## Research Direction

```text
Optical Sensing
│
├── Fabry–Pérot Microcavity
│   ├── Resonant modes
│   ├── Optical gain and loss
│   └── Laser-output characterization
│
├── Chemical Response
│   ├── HRP-catalyzed TMB reaction
│   ├── Absorption modulation
│   └── Sulfide-ion inhibition
│
└── Signal Processing
    ├── Spectral analysis
    ├── Threshold fitting
    ├── Concentration calibration
    └── Scientific visualization
```
